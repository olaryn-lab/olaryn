Olaryn Analytics

Shared product and website measurement across Olaryn.

Shared service · Used by Olaryn products · Not a public SaaS product

Purpose

Olaryn Analytics brings together the basic signals our teams use to understand how products are discovered and used. It supports product decisions and operational reporting rather than being a separate commercial offering.

Responsibilities

Acquisition
Visits and traffic sources across product websites.
Product activity
Recorded actions that help us understand product use.
Usage insights
Usage trends used to guide prioritization and improvement.

Service boundary

The analytics service operates at analytics.olaryn.com. This page is a descriptive overview; it does not expose the reporting interface or grant access to product data.

Access to internal dashboards is managed separately. No public analytics signup is available.

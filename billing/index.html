Olaryn Billing

Subscriptions, credits, and usage accounting for Olaryn products.

Shared service · Used by Olaryn products · Not a public SaaS product

Purpose

Olaryn Billing centralizes the mechanics of charging for recurring plans and metered work. Product teams define what their work costs; a shared service records usage and keeps balances consistent.

Responsibilities

Plans and payments
Subscriptions and payment workflows, integrated with Stripe.
Usage and entitlements
Credit and usage tracking, integrated with OpenMeter.
Reservation and settlement
Reserve credits before eligible work, settle actual usage, and return unused credits.

Service boundary

The billing service operates at billing.olaryn.com. It is an internal API for Olaryn products, not a public checkout, developer signup, or standalone SaaS offer.

Billing integrations are controlled within our products. Access and requests are scoped through the shared identity infrastructure.

What the code enforces

Enforced by the service, not left to the caller.

Competing holds are serializedTwo 25-credit holds against 35 credits: exactly one succeeds.
Holds expireAn unsettled hold lapses after its TTL, 15 minutes by default, and the credits return.
Retries are safePaid actions take an Idempotency-Key of 8 to 255 characters. Reuse it when retrying.
Access follows paymentStripe plans and allowances change only on a verified paid invoice.
Refunds reverse creditsMatched grants are clawed back on a refund, dispute or void.
Metering lag cannot be spentUsage settled here but not yet seen by OpenMeter is subtracted from the balance.

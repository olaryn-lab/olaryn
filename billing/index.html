The service boundary

Move the money logic out of every product.

Three systems, three responsibilities. Product teams own what an operation costs. Stripe handles financial transactions. OpenMeter tracks credits and entitlements. Billing coordinates them through one authenticated service.

01 / PRODUCT

Determine the cost

The application understands the feature, usage unit, and credit amount. It asks Billing to authorize the operation before running it.

02 / COMMERCIAL

Coordinate financial state

Billing orchestrates subscriptions, credit purchases, customer bindings, verified webhooks, and manual commercial arrangements.

03 / ACCESS

Keep access consistent

Credit reservations, settlements, grants, and entitlements are applied through OpenMeter with reconciliation and audit trails.

Platform capabilities

Commercial plumbing, handled once.

Instead of building slightly different billing and credit systems in every application, each product integrates with the same commercial API.

01 / CATALOG

Plans & add-ons

Centralize commercial catalogs, recurring allowances, and purchased credit packs for each product.

02 / MONEY

Subscriptions & payments

Use Stripe for checkout, invoicing, payment methods, lifecycle changes, refunds, and disputes.

03 / USAGE

Reserve, settle, release

Protect available credits with idempotent reservations and account for completed or cancelled operations.

04 / ACCESS

Credits & entitlements

Use OpenMeter as the authority for effective access, credit grants, balances, and metered usage.

05 / OPERATIONS

Manual administration

Support operator-granted credits, custom contracts, lifecycle changes, and auditable commercial actions.

06 / SECURITY

Product isolation

Verify Authward-issued machine credentials and scope access to the correct product and resources.

The API contract

Reserve before work. Settle after.

Your product calculates the cost and asks Billing to reserve it. When the work finishes you settle the reservation; if it is cancelled you release it. A stable idempotency key makes retries safe, and reservations expire on their own.

Calls are authenticated with an Authward-issued machine token and scoped to your product.

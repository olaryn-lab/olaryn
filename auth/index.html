What happens when someone signs in
  1. They pick a way to sign inOn your screen, with your name and colours.
  2. Zikela checks it’s really themFingerprint or face, a link in their email, or their Google or GitHub account.
  3. They’re back in your app, signed inYour app gets a short sign-in pass that tells it who they are.

Sign-in passes last 15 minutes and can be cancelled.

For developersstandard sign-in, nothing special to learn

Zikela speaks OAuth and OpenID Connect, so any library that supports them will work. No special kit to install.

  • Works with common librariesStandard OAuth and OpenID Connect, with PKCE required.
  • Command-line tools tooPeople can sign in to a CLI by approving it on another device.
  • Safe by defaultSigning keys change every 30 days, and sign-ins only return to addresses you registered.

Olaryn Identity

Authentication and access shared across Olaryn products.

Shared service · Used by Olaryn products · Not a public SaaS product

Purpose

Olaryn Identity provides a common account and sign-in foundation for the software we operate. Each product keeps its own experience while relying on shared identity infrastructure.

Responsibilities

Sign-in
Accounts, sessions, and supported sign-in methods.
Organizations
Organization membership, invitations, and access management.
Identity protocols
OAuth/OIDC authentication and authorization for connected products.

Service boundary

The identity service and OAuth/OIDC issuer run at auth.olaryn.com. This page is a company overview, not an issuer, sign-in endpoint, or protocol callback.

Product teams integrate with the issuer and its documented endpoints, not with this public overview.
